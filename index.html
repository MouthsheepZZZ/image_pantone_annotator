📖 How to Use

  1. Upload an Image: Click the upload area or drag and drop your image file
  2. Click to Extract Colors: Click anywhere on the image to extract the color at that point
  3. View Pantone Match: The closest matching Pantone color will be displayed with a color swatch and code
  4. Drag to Reposition: Click and drag any color swatch or sampling point to move it freely
  5. Filter Colors: Click "Color Filter" to select specific Pantone systems and card types for matching
  6. Customize Display: Click "Settings" to adjust swatch size and font sizes to your preference
  7. Add Multiple Points: Click multiple locations to create a color palette from your image
  8. Export Image: Click "Export Image" to download the annotated image with all color labels
  9. Export JSON: Click "Export JSON" to save color point data with coordinates for later use
  10. Import JSON: Click "Import JSON" to restore previously saved color points and their positions
